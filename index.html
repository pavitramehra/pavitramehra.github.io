About

I'm a Software Engineer at Amazon Web Services (AWS), working on the Device Farm team where I build distributed, event-driven systems to enable large-scale testing of mobile applications on real devices. My work focuses on designing high-throughput, reliable backend systems using serverless and cloud-native technologies.

Recently, I have been building a low-latency H.264 streaming system for remote device access across Android and iOS, optimizing video pipelines for real-time performance in low-bandwidth environments. This includes end-to-end system design spanning device capture (ADB/WDA), encoding (FFmpeg), and scalable backend services.

I am also interested in the intersection of distributed systems and AI, and have worked on applying Large Language Models for data cleaning and preprocessing (IEEE BigData 2024). More recently, I’ve been exploring agentic workflows and MCP-based systems for building tool-augmented AI applications.

I enjoy building systems that operate at scale and under real-world constraints, particularly in areas like cloud infrastructure, media streaming, and intelligent automation.
